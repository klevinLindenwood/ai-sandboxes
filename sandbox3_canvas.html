🔍 AI Sandbox 3 — Bias & Equity Analyzer

Paste an assessment item to receive an AI analysis of potential bias, accessibility, and equity concerns.

⚠️ Important limitation: AI bias analysis is a starting point for your own thinking — not a definitive audit. It may identify issues that aren't real problems and miss issues that are. Always combine AI analysis with your own professional judgment and, ideally, diverse human colleague review.

This sandbox analyzes assessment items across five equity dimensions:

🌍 Cultural Bias Assumes familiarity with specific cultural contexts that may advantage some students
🗣 Linguistic Bias Vocabulary or structures that disadvantage ELL or non-mainstream dialect speakers
💰 Socioeconomic Bias Assumes access to experiences or resources not universally available
👥 Representation Stereotyping, underrepresentation, or limiting portrayals of any group
♿ Accessibility Barriers for students with reading disabilities, visual impairments, or other differences unrelated to the content being assessed
